2. What data we process

This website is a presentation website and does not contain user accounts or a server-side contact form. If you contact us by e-mail, we process the information included in your message, such as your name, e-mail address and the content of your enquiry, so that we can respond and, where appropriate, prepare an offer.

When the website is accessed, hosting and security systems may process technical information needed to deliver the content and protect the service, such as IP address, request time, URL and browser information.

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • Website operation and security: ensuring availability, correct operation and protection against abuse; the legal basis is our legitimate interest in operating a secure service.
  • Handling an enquiry: responding to a message and preparing potential cooperation; the legal basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract or performing a contract.
  • Audience analytics: measuring website use with Google Analytics; the legal basis is your prior consent.

4. Cookies and local storage

The website also uses technologies similar to cookies, such as local storage to remember the selected colour theme. This is a user-interface preference and is not used for analytics or marketing.

Analytics cookies are activated only after you actively consent through the cookie banner. If you reject consent or make no choice, Google Analytics is not started for analytics purposes.

5. Google Analytics and Cloudflare

After consent, we use Google Analytics 4 to evaluate website traffic. The tool is technically delivered through Cloudflare Zaraz. Analytics is not used to directly identify you or to display personalised advertising.

In connection with website operation, technical data may be processed by Cloudflare and Google. Depending on the configuration, data may be processed or transferred outside the European Economic Area; the providers use the applicable legal safeguards described in their current documentation.

6. External resources

The website loads fonts from Google Fonts. This request is not used for analytics, but the browser may send technical information required to deliver the resource, such as an IP address. If minimising external requests becomes a priority, the fonts can be hosted directly on this website in the future.

7. Your choices and rights

Consent to analytics cookies is voluntary, and refusing it does not affect access to the website content. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time using the Cookie settings link in the website footer. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

Subject to the conditions set by the GDPR, you may request access to your personal data, rectification, erasure or restriction of processing, object to processing, or request data portability. You may also lodge a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection.

8. Updates

This information may be updated when the technologies used, analytics tools or legal requirements change. The current version is always published on this page.
